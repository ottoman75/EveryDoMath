2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Mental-arithmetic practice for elementary school children (grades 1-6) and
their parents. A session is 20 problems generated on the device for the
child's grade (G1-G2 add and subtract, G3 adds multiplication, G4 adds
division, G5-G6 fractions and decimals). Problem solved: daily drilling is
repetitive for the child and invisible to the parent, who cannot tell which
operation the child is weak at. Value: a session takes about two minutes, the
child gets immediate feedback and a score, and the parent dashboard shows
accuracy per operation and the streak. All grades free; no ads, no IAP.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
No login credentials or sample files are needed; the app has no account
system. On first launch it signs in to Firebase Anonymous Authentication in
the background, and the child only enters a nickname and picks a grade.
The interface is Korean, so the exact button text is given:
- Home: 시작하기 starts a session. At the bottom, 리더보드 (Leaderboard) and
  가족 리더보드 (Family Leaderboard). Above them 부모 대시보드 (Parent
  Dashboard), which is behind the gate.
- Profile: the round PERSON ICON at the TOP-RIGHT of Home (icon only, no text
  label). It contains 데이터 초기화 (Reset Data), which erases local records
  and also deletes the public leaderboard entry and family-group membership.
- Moderation: in 리더보드, press and hold about one second on a row that is
  NOT your own. A menu opens with 신고하기 (Report) and 안 보이게 하기
  (Hide/block). Your own row shows no menu, by design.
- PARENT GATE: it shows a two-digit multiplication written in Korean words,
  for example "십사 곱하기 십칠". Both numbers are between 11 and 19:
  십일=11, 십이=12, 십삼=13, 십사=14, 십오=15, 십육=16, 십칠=17, 십팔=18,
  십구=19. So that example is 14 x 17 = 238. Type the product and tap 확인.
  A wrong answer simply generates a new question.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Only two, both from Firebase: Anonymous Authentication (issues an anonymous id
so the server can verify who writes a leaderboard record; no email, phone or
social login) and Cloud Firestore (leaderboard entry, family group, nickname
reports). Linked libraries are exactly FirebaseCore, FirebaseAuth and
FirebaseFirestore. There is no analytics SDK, crash reporter, advertising SDK,
payment processor, AI service or third-party data provider. Problems are
generated on device, so play works offline; only the leaderboard and family
group need a network.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions consistently across all regions: the same grades, the
same problem generator, the same features and one shared global leaderboard.
The UI is localized in Korean, English and Japanese following the device
language; the arithmetic content is language-independent. Nothing is gated by
country.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY OR PROTECTED THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Neither applies, so no documentation or credentials are needed. Every problem
is generated algorithmically at runtime, not taken from a textbook or question
bank. The illustrations, icon and copy are the developer's original work.
