EveryDoMath (매일매일 수학) 1.0 — App Review Notes

[1] WHAT THE APP DOES AND WHO IT IS FOR
A mental-arithmetic drill app for elementary school children (grades 1-6) and
their parents. One session is 20 problems generated on the device, scoped to the
child's grade:
  Grade 1  + and - within 20
  Grade 2  + and - within 100
  Grade 3  + , - , x (1-9 times tables)
  Grade 4  + , - , x (2-digit x 1-digit), division without remainder
  Grade 5  fraction + and -, decimal + and - (mixed)
  Grade 6  fraction x and division, decimal x and division
Problem it solves: daily arithmetic practice is repetitive, and parents cannot
see where a child is actually weak. A session takes about two minutes, the child
gets immediate feedback and a score, and a parent can open the Parent Dashboard
to see accuracy per operation and the current streak. All six grades are free.
There is no advertising, no in-app purchase, and no subscription.

[2] NO ACCOUNT, NO LOGIN, NO CREDENTIALS NEEDED
Nothing is registered and no demo account is required to review any part of the
app. On first launch the app signs in to Firebase Anonymous Authentication in
the background. The child only types a nickname (2-12 characters) and picks a
grade. Because there is no account creation, there is no account deletion flow.
Profile > "데이터 초기화" (Reset Data) erases all local records and also deletes
the user's public leaderboard entry and family-group membership on the server.

[3] HOW TO REACH EACH FEATURE
Home      tap the large start button to play. The Daily Challenge card and the
          daily goal progress bar are on the same screen.
Game      20 problems, on-screen number pad, per-problem timer ring.
Result    score, grade (S/A/B/C/D), max combo, and a shareable result card.
Leaderboard  Home > 랭킹. Global top 50, filterable by grade.
          To report or hide an entry, LONG-PRESS any row that is not your own.
          A context menu appears with 신고 (report) and 차단 (hide).
Profile   Home > 프로필. Achievements, streak, and 데이터 초기화 (Reset Data).
Parent    Home > 부모 대시보드, behind a parent gate (below). Tabs: dashboard
          (accuracy per operation), 목표 설정 (daily goal and study reminder),
          가족 랭킹 (family group joined by a 6-character invite code).

[PARENT GATE — PLEASE READ BEFORE TESTING]
The Parent Dashboard is behind a gate that shows a two-digit multiplication
written in Korean words, for example "십사 곱하기 십칠". Both numbers are
between 11 and 19. Korean number words: 십일=11, 십이=12, 십삼=13, 십사=14,
십오=15, 십육=16, 십칠=17, 십팔=18, 십구=19.
So "십사 곱하기 십칠" = 14 x 17 = 238. Type the product and tap 확인.
A wrong answer generates a new question.

[4] EXTERNAL SERVICES USED
Only two, both from Firebase (Google):
  - Firebase Anonymous Authentication: issues an anonymous user id so the server
    can verify who is writing a leaderboard record. No email, phone number or
    social login is involved.
  - Cloud Firestore: stores the global leaderboard entry (nickname, grade,
    score, correct count), family-group membership, and nickname reports.
Linked Firebase libraries are exactly FirebaseCore, FirebaseAuth and
FirebaseFirestore. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising
SDK, no payment processor, no AI or machine-learning service, and no third-party
data provider. All math problems are generated by on-device code
(ProblemGenerator), so solving problems works fully offline. Only the
leaderboard and the family group need a network connection.

[5] REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app behaves identically in every region: the same six grades, the same
problem generator, the same features, and one shared global leaderboard. The
interface is localized into Korean, English and Japanese and follows the device
language; the arithmetic content itself is language-independent. No feature,
price or content is gated by country.

[6] REGULATED INDUSTRY OR THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Neither applies. This is not a regulated industry, and the app contains no
licensed or protected third-party material. Every math problem is produced
algorithmically at runtime, not taken from a textbook or question bank. The
illustrations, icon and copy are original work by the developer. No trademarks,
curricula or content owned by a school, publisher or institution are used.

[USER-GENERATED CONTENT — GUIDELINE 1.2]
The leaderboard shows nicknames chosen by users. Safeguards:
  - Nickname validation blocks banned words and strings that look like personal
    information (4+ consecutive digits). A notice on the nickname screen tells
    users not to enter real names, school names or phone numbers.
  - Any leaderboard entry can be reported (신고) or hidden (차단) by
    long-pressing the entry, as described in section 3.
  - Firestore security rules prevent a user from reporting themselves,
    impersonating another reporter, or reading the reports collection.

[NOTIFICATIONS]
The app does not ask for notification permission on first launch. Permission is
requested only when a parent turns on 학습 알림 in Parent Dashboard > 목표 설정.
If permission is denied, the toggle returns to off.

[SCOPE]
iPhone only; iPad layout is not supported in 1.0.
