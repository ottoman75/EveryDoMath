Thank you for keeping the submission open. Our previous fix addressed a real
bug but not the one you were seeing, and we are sorry for the extra round. We
have now found the actual cause and made the app a true iPad app.

WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG
The app was built for iPhone only (TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1). On your review
device it therefore ran in iPhone compatibility mode, and the accessibility
tree confirmed what that meant in practice: the app window measured exactly
375 x 667 points on an iPad Air. The whole interface was being drawn on an
iPhone 8 canvas and then scaled up to fill the iPad. That is why text looked
soft, the grade selector was clipped so "6학년" could not be seen, and a large
band of empty space sat between the grade row and the Start button. It matches
your wording precisely: not optimized for all screen sizes OR RESOLUTIONS.

Our previous change (locking iPhone to portrait) fixed a genuine defect in
landscape, but it could not fix this, because the canvas size was the problem.

WHAT WE CHANGED
1. The app is now a universal app. TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY is "1,2", so on iPad
   it receives the native iPad canvas and renders at native resolution instead
   of being upscaled.
2. iPad supports all four orientations. iPadOS lets the user resize the window
   freely, so the layout is built to tolerate arbitrary sizes rather than
   relying on a fixed one.
3. Layout work for large canvases. Content is capped to a readable width and
   centred instead of stretching edge to edge, and on screens taller than the
   content the layout is centred vertically rather than pinned to the top. The
   grade selector now shows all six grades without clipping.
4. iPhone layout is deliberately untouched. The width cap applies only to the
   regular size class; we verified that the iPhone screens are unchanged.

HOW WE VERIFIED IT
- Automated UI tests on iPhone 18 Pro, iPad Pro 13-inch and iPad Air 11-inch,
  the same family as your review device. All pass.
- One of those tests asserts specifically that the app window on iPad is NOT
  375 x 667, so the compatibility-canvas regression cannot come back unnoticed.
- The rotation test asserts that the primary button is hittable, not merely
  present, after rotating in both landscape directions.
- The full screenshot walkthrough (setup, home, game, result, leaderboard,
  parent dashboard) now runs to completion on iPad Pro 13-inch as well as on
  iPhone.
- Unit tests: 14 passed, 0 failures.
